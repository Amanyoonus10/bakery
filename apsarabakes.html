Apsara Bakes Private Limited — Full Profile

OUR JOURNEY

From Homemade Supply to Kannur's Premier Brand

Established by Mr. K. V. Mayan in the 1980s at Iritty, Kannur. Apsara Bakes began as a humble homemade bakery supply business. Driven by quality, hygiene & authentic taste, it has grown into Kannur's premier bakery icon operating 17 modern retail outlets serving over 10 Lakh+ satisfied customers.

1000+ Products & Artisanal Offerings Exports to New Zealand, Australia, GCC, Oman & USA (5+ Countries) Dedicated Fresh Production Facilities

FOOD SAFETY MANAGEMENT

ISO 22000:2005 Certified Cleanroom Standard

Certified Food Safety System ensuring the highest standards of cleanliness, hygiene, and quality control across all baking units. Features 100% hygienic facilities, automated slicing, touchless baking protocols, and nitrogen vacuum sealing for ultimate freshness.

Cleanroom Facilities Automated Touchless Baking Nitrogen Vacuum Sealing

OUR VISION

World-Class Quality & Culinary Innovation

To become the most trusted and preferred bakery brand across Kerala and international markets by delivering world-class quality, continuous culinary innovation, authentic traditional heritage recipes, and uncompromised customer satisfaction.

OUR MISSION

Taste the Best Always — Daily Excellence

To deliver fresh, hygienic, and premium-quality bakery products while continuously elevating our recipes, production processes, environmental standards, and customer joy across every single outlet daily.
